AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made and entered into this 15th day of July, 2023 by and between:

ABC Corporation

123 Business Avenue

New York, NY 10001

Hereinafter referred to as "Party A"

XYZ Services LLC

456 Commerce Street

Boston, MA 02108

Hereinafter referred to as "Party B"

Whereas, the parties desire to enter into this Agreement to set forth the terms and conditions of their arrangement.

1. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms under which Party B will provide marketing services to Party A.

2. Terms

Party B agrees to provide the following services: Social media management, content creation, and monthly analytics reporting. Party A agrees to provide all necessary brand assets and timely feedback on deliverables.

3. Duration

This Agreement shall commence on July 15, 2023 and shall continue for a period of twelve (12) months unless terminated earlier according to Section 5.

4. Compensation

Party A shall pay Party B $2,500 per month, payable on the first business day of each month. Payment shall be made via bank transfer to the account specified by Party B.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. In the event of termination, Party B shall be compensated for all services rendered up to the termination date.

6. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York.

7. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.

Party A
